Six drills. Press a number to start one.

Each drill is built around a fight that actually happens in raid, scored so you can see yourself getting faster.

1

Head, eyes

One-tap head flicks between Customs containers. Speed and first-shot accuracy.

2

Corner peek

Jiggle- and swing-peeks from cover, with a shooting window that keeps shrinking.

3

ADAD tracking

A strafing, crouch-spamming PMC. Hold fire and stay on him.

4

Spray control

M4A1, AK-74N, AKM and MP7 recoil, with a grouping plot after every spray.

5

CQB switch

Three close targets. A head takes one tap, a thorax takes two.

6

Long shot

2x to 6x optics with sway and hold breath, on walkers 80 to 200 metres out.

Controls work like the game: RMB to aim (tap toggles, hold holds), WASD to walk, Shift to hold breath, R to reload, Esc to pause, F11 for fullscreen.

Running in under a minute

  1. Download the exe

    One file, RaidReady.exe. No installer. It opens in its own window using Edge, or Chrome and Brave if you have them.

  2. Get past SmartScreen

    The exe isn't code-signed, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Match your sens

    Click Match my Tarkov sens and enter your DPI, mouse and aiming sensitivity, and FOV. Then pick a drill.
