RAID//READY — Tarkov Aim Trainer
================================

RUN IT
  Double-click RaidReady.exe. It opens in its own window (uses Microsoft Edge,
  which every Windows 10/11 PC has; falls back to Chrome/Brave).
  First launch: Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because
  the exe isn't code-signed. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

FIRST THING: MATCH YOUR SENS
  Click "Match my Tarkov sens" and enter:
    - Mouse DPI (from your mouse software)
    - Mouse sensitivity        (Tarkov > Settings > Controls)
    - Aiming sensitivity       (Tarkov > Settings > Controls)
    - FOV                      (Tarkov > Settings > Game)
  The trainer uses raw mouse input and Tarkov's 0.113636 deg/count rotation,
  so the same swipe turns you the same distance as in raid.
  (If BSG ever changes the formula you can edit the yaw under Advanced.)

DRILLS
  1 HEAD, EYES     one-tap head flicks, Customs containers
  2 CORNER PEEK    jiggle- and swing-peeks from cover, shrinking window
  3 ADAD TRACKING  strafing + crouch-spamming PMC, hold fire
  4 SPRAY CONTROL  M4A1 / AK-74N / AKM / MP7 recoil, grouping plot after
  5 CQB SWITCH     three close targets; head = 1 tap, thorax = 2
  6 LONG SHOT      2x–6x optic, sway, hold breath, walkers at 80–200m
  RAID WARM-UP     drills 1-4 back to back, 45s each (~3 min) —
                   start it when you hit "Ready" on the raid loading screen.

CONTROLS
  LMB fire · RMB ADS (tap = toggle, hold = hold, like Tarkov)
  SHIFT hold breath · R reload · ESC pause · F11 fullscreen · 1-6 quick start

YOUR DATA
  Settings, PBs and history: %APPDATA%\RaidReady\data.json

Ranks: TIMMY > SCAV > PMC > RAIDER > ROGUE > BOSS
